# How autopay works

Autopay takes your scheduled payment automatically from a linked bank account on each due date. The amount taken is the amount due for that month.

To turn autopay on or off, we first confirm your identity with a one-time code sent by text message to the phone number on file. Changes take effect from the next due date. If a due date is less than two business days away, that payment may still be taken under the old setting.
